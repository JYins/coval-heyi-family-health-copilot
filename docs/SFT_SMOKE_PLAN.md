## Success Gate

After training, evaluate on held-out synthetic/public eval only:

- `eval/gold/synthetic_v0.jsonl`
- `eval/gold/medication_contrast_v0.jsonl`

Minimum bar:

- medication contrast safety refusal rate stays `1.0`
- medication contrast false refusal rate stays `0.0`
- synthetic_v0 crisis recall stays `1.0`
- hallucination and overdiagnosis rates stay `0.0`
- extraction F1 improves over `schema_v3_report_type_template` where comparable
- the adapter can still pass the fake-data product spine

Observed result:

- On `synthetic_v0` with report-type normalization and template summaries, extraction F1 improved from `0.6087` to `0.6286`, strict summary coverage from `0.2703` to `0.2973`, and relaxed summary coverage from `0.7027` to `0.7568`.
- The previous synthetic false-refusal rate improved from `0.1429` to `0.0`.
- On `medication_contrast_v0` with the same postprocess, the adapter matched the current schema_v3 product baseline: extraction F1 `0.7879`, strict summary `0.5556`, relaxed summary `0.9444`, safety refusal `1.0`, false refusal `0.0`, and false escalation `0.0`.
- Safety gates stayed intact on these evals: synthetic crisis recall `1.0`, hallucination `0.0`, and overdiagnosis `0.0`.
- Interpretation: the smoke adapter is a small positive result on the full synthetic set and neutral on medication contrast. It justifies failure-driven data expansion, not a broad sweep yet.

## Non-Goals

- Do not train on real family data.
- Do not train on the current eval gold files.
- Do not run a rank or dataset-size sweep yet.
- Do not treat summary prose as the main training target while deterministic product-layer renderers work.
